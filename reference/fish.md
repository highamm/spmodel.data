# Fish Richness in Rivers and Streams in 2018 in U.S.

Fish species richness data from a subset of an Environmental Protection
Agency National Rivers and Streams Assessment (NRSA) program in the year
2018.

## Usage

``` r
fish
```

## Format

An `sf` object with 104 rows and 3 columns:

- fish_richness: the number of unique species of fish in the stream.

- mean_temp: the average annual air temperature, in degrees Celsius.

- geometry: a point location of the measurements.
